Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. Who we are

Neural Jolt is a YouTube channel about AI news (youtube.com/@NeuralJolt). This policy covers this website and Neural Jolt Publisher, the desktop application we use to publish and manage videos on our channel. Contact: dragostream.ttv@gmail.com.

2. What Neural Jolt Publisher does

Neural Jolt Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Neural Jolt channel. After the owner signs in with Google and grants access, it uses the YouTube Data API v3 to upload our videos and manage their information on the Neural Jolt channel. It is not offered to other people and it never accesses anyone else's account.

3. Google user data we access

When the channel owner signs in, the app asks for these permissions (OAuth scopes):

  • youtube.upload: upload videos to the channel;
  • youtube: manage the channel's videos and playlists and its settings (description, keywords, banner);
  • youtube.force-ssl: manage captions and the same content over a secure connection.

With them, the app reads and updates only: the channel's basic information (name, ID, handle and branding settings), the videos we upload and their information (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, category, privacy and publishing schedule, processing status) and the channel's playlists. It does not read viewers' personal data, comments, subscriptions or watch history.

4. How we use it

Only to publish and manage videos on the Neural Jolt channel, as requested by the channel owner. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

5. Storage and security

The access and refresh tokens that Google issues are stored only on the channel owner's computer, in a local file that is never uploaded or shared. The app has no server and no database: videos and their information stay on the owner's computer and are sent only to YouTube, always over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

6. Sharing

We do not share Google user data with anyone. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, through the YouTube API, to perform the actions described above.

7. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions); the local token file is then deleted. Videos and information published on YouTube are managed on YouTube under its own policies. To ask about or request the deletion of any data, write to dragostream.ttv@gmail.com.

8. YouTube API Services

Neural Jolt Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data processed by Google is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Limited Use

Neural Jolt Publisher's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. This website

This website is a set of static pages: no cookies, no analytics, no forms, no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

11. Viewers of our videos

Watching, liking, commenting on or subscribing to our videos happens on YouTube and is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. We only see the aggregated statistics and the public comments that YouTube shows to every creator.

12. Children

Our channel, this website and our tools are not directed to children under 13.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the effective date at the top of this page.
